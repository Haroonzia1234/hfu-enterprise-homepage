# Contest Submission: HFU Enterprise Homepage

## Design Approach

We structured this concept around the "Route Board" identity, inspired directly by HFU's forward chevron. The chevron serves as our singular decorative motif across arrows, list markers, and map pins. Rather than burying enquiry forms behind navigation layers, we made the interactive route planner the hero. High-contrast dark "ink boards" house every operational tool - the UK journey map, fleet selector, weekly run builder, and coverage radar - contrasting cleanly against warm paper backgrounds for reading sections.

## Why It Converts

Every interaction is designed to eliminate booking friction. The hero route planner immediately captures origin, destination, service level, and pallet volume, passing this state seamlessly into the quote form so customers never re-enter details. Mobile visitors benefit from a persistent sticky bar offering one-tap calling and instant quote navigation. HFU's 15-minute response promise is reinforced at critical decision points, while office lines, mobile numbers, and dispatch emails remain accessible on every viewport.

## Tailored to HFU

This concept rejects generic logistics templates by anchoring every component in HFU's operational reality:

- All eight vehicles from Small Van to 26 Tonne rigid truck feature authentic dimensions, payload limits, and pallet capacities.
- Real booking hours reflect collection slots available every 15 minutes between 4:15 am and 11:45 pm.
- Coverage highlights HFU's six core Greater Manchester areas (Manchester, Salford, Stockport, Trafford, Didsbury, Chorlton) alongside national transit.
- HFU's seven core courier and haulage services are individually profiled with specific operational details.

## Platform Recommendation for the Full Site

Because HFU already runs WordPress, we recommend deploying a custom lightweight WordPress theme paired with a dedicated quote plugin. This allows the team to edit content and fleet specifications easily while the plugin records quote enquiries and emails dispatch immediately. Full conversion tracking is built in using twelve standardized dataLayer events for GA4 and Google Tag Manager. The architecture ensures excellent Core Web Vitals with zero runtime dependencies and provides solid SEO foundations: a single semantic H1, automated JSON-LD schemas (LocalBusiness and FAQPage), and structured templates for future dedicated service landing pages.

## Current Placeholders

To remain strictly transparent about what requires client input:

- Accreditations, insurance cover figures, and client logos were not supplied on the live site and are omitted rather than invented.
- The quote form currently simulates submission client-side until connected to a production API endpoint.
- A compliant privacy policy and terms page must be linked before production launch.

We welcome your feedback and look forward to refining this concept together.
